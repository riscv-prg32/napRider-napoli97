# Notice

`napRider-napoli97` is an unofficial fan/tribute project.

The name is a wordplay on Napoli (`nap`) and the talking-car action genre. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the owners of *Knight Rider*, Fiat/Stellantis, the City of Naples, Napoli Sotterranea, or custodians of the Virgil/Castel dell'Ovo traditions.

The game contains no Knight Rider logos, character names, dialogue, soundtrack recording, or copied theme melody. The white 1971 Fiat 500 L is depicted as a period vehicle reference; no manufacturer logo is required for gameplay.

The Virgil's Egg story is a fictional game adaptation of a long-standing Neapolitan legend. Real locations are stylized for gameplay and should not be read as an archaeological or historical reconstruction.
