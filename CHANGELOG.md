# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — 2026-10-03 — indexed colours, palette effects, SID-like stereo

### Fixed

- The Store cartridge was 76.9 KiB, over the 64 KiB package limit of current PRG32 firmware, so the default ESP32-C6 and QEMU builds refused it. It is now about 36 KiB and needs 24 KiB of cartridge RAM instead of a 64 KiB or 128 KiB profile.
- The build used a private patch of the PRG32 builder that asserted a 32 KiB fallback; PRG32 `main` no longer has it, so the build failed. It now uses the stock CLI.
- Colours on the ESP32-C6 were quantised to the firmware's 216-colour cube (the 128-colour palette collapsed to 30 colours). The cartridge now programs the system palette so the board shows the authored colours, identical to QEMU.
- The music was written for an older tracker that charged one tick per event; on current firmware every note was switched off in the tick it started. The score is regenerated for delta-after timing.
- Turbo and gadget sounds were started and never released, leaving a note droning on their voice.
- Running out of fuel froze the car for ever with no way out. It now ends the run.
- Scooters could not touch the car, and the headlights and battery did nothing but count down.
- Pressing `SELECT` before `B` for the headlights also toggled the handbrake. Self-drive and handbrake now act on release, the combination on press.
- The car and scooter sprites were ragged crops of the reference sheet; the scooters were unrecognisable debris.

### Graphics

- All art is now drawn pixel by pixel by `tools/generate_assets.py`: a shared 26-tile bank at 4 bpp with a 16-colour palette per district, a side-view Fiat 500 L, three palette-swapped scooter gangs, the Egg, Vesuvius, an island and a headlight beam. About 9 KiB, down from 43 KiB.
- New district scenery: tenements with washing lines and shops, the Posillipo seawall and gulf, umbrella pines, the Galleria at night, ruins at the Parco Virgiliano, tuff vaults with arches and torches.
- Everything is drawn through the palette-indexed calls; the HUD is redrawn only when it changes and the screen is no longer cleared every frame.
- Palette effects: fades between districts, the unlit tunnel, a flash on collecting a fragment, shimmering sea, flickering torches, the glowing Egg, a sky gradient per district.
- Headlight beam, turbo flame, screen shake and crash blinking.

### Audio

- Ten SID-like procedural instruments and eight original tracks (six districts, the Egg, game over), generated by `tools/generate_audio.py`.
- Effects on four dedicated voices, panned by screen position: speed-following engine, scooters, turbo, gadgets, crash, fragment, petrol, and a voice for the car's phrases.

### Game

- Title screen with the record, a card introducing each district, an ending screen and a game-over screen.
- Districts are nine screens long; the simulation follows real time (`prg32_ticks_ms`), so a slow frame does not slow the car.
- Scooter gangs block and chase; collisions cost fuel and speed. Self-drive dodges and leaves the gadgets to the player.
- Petrol pumps on the pavement, a battery that recharges while driving unlit, rubble underground.
- Scores go to the firmware's local top five.

### Tooling

- `tests/run_harness.sh`: a bot plays the whole campaign on a model of both display back ends and fails on any pixel that differs between them.
- `tools/qemu_capture.py`: records the cartridge in the QEMU firmware (frames from guest memory, real UART audio) without screen-recording permission.
- `build.sh` validates the bundle with the Cartridge Store's own intake code; `dist/` is now committed.
- Removed the reference-sheet crop pipeline and the builder/uploader patches.

## 1.0.1 — unreleased fixes folded into 2.0.0

- Fixed the road's left-edge row scrolling at the district (one-quarter) parallax speed instead of full road speed, which drifted it out of sync with the rest of the roadway and with fragment/collision detection.
- Fixed `SELECT+B` (toggle headlights) not registering when `SELECT` is pressed and held before `B`; only the reverse press order worked previously.
- Corrected stale documentation: tile bank is 72 frames (not 66), the game is horizontal-parallax (not isometric), and the asset pipeline has no scikit-learn dependency.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-13

- Renamed the saga entry to **napRider-napoli97**.
- Retargeted the project to PRG32 `main` portable ABI build flow.
- Replaced the San Gennaro treasure campaign with a mythological Virgil's Egg storyline.
- Added Napoli Sotterranea as the final dungeon-like stage and a two-choice ending.
- Converted runtime art to 8-bpp indexed sprites and a 72-frame semantic tile bank with byte-coded Naples district maps.
- Preserved the recognizable white 1971 Fiat 500 L as dedicated pseudo-3D directional art.
- Retained manual/self-drive, turbo, handbrake, fuel/battery/lights, scooter gangs and arcade gadgets.
- Added GitHub Actions, Store metadata, reproducible asset tooling and strict source checks.
